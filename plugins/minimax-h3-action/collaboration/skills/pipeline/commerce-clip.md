# Single Commerce Clip

Design one ecommerce sales clip at the user's exact duration, not a full advertisement or editing plan. Use one real product, one supported selling point, one visible proof and at most one conversion action.

## Priorities

- Open with the product already solving a problem or producing the desired result. Do not open on a logo, greeting or slow empty reveal.
- Use one structure: `need/result hook -> product action -> visible proof -> optional single CTA`.
- Keep the actual product continuously recognizable. Lock shape, proportions, color, material, packaging, controls and distinctive details from references.
- A benefit must be proven by a visible product interaction or observable outcome. Do not convert unsupported adjectives into factual claims.
- Favor clear hands-on use, a feature close-up and honest color over spectacle. Camera motion supports the proof and does not hide the product.
- Keep voice concise and synchronized with the demonstrated action. Product foley occurs at the physical cause; music remains secondary.
- If the user explicitly supplies a CTA, preserve one. Otherwise end on the demonstrated result or clean product hold.

## Guardrails

- Never invent price, discount, specification, certification, performance number, health claim or comparison.
- Do not ask the video model to render prices, captions, logos or promotional copy. Deterministic text and branding belong to external post-production.
- Do not redesign the product, add variants, exaggerate before/after results or show an unsupported use case.
- Do not turn this into a premium abstract hero reveal; use `product_showcase` when visual prestige rather than sales proof is the main purpose.

Source basis: TikTok Shop guidance on showing the actual product and factual demonstrations; Shopify product-demo structure; Amazon product realism guidance. The single-selling-point constraint is gateway synthesis for short H3 generation clips.
