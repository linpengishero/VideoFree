# Emotional Close-up Clip

Design one observable emotional change around a concrete stimulus. When duration permits, use `stimulus -> recognition -> brief involuntary leakage -> attempted control or release -> residual behavior` without diagnosing inner psychology. For 1-3 seconds, show one causally clear fragment such as a glance, caught breath, leakage or residual reaction rather than forcing the full arc.

- Use only one or two compatible visible signals per beat: gaze shift, interrupted blink, eyelid pressure, asymmetric mouth tension, jaw set, swallow, caught breath, fingers tightening or moisture gathering.
- Strong emotion develops from the supplied state; it does not begin at maximum intensity unless explicitly established.
- Frame close enough to see the decisive signal while preserving enough posture or context to understand its cause.
- Prefer `continuous`. Use one motivated cut only when the stimulus and reaction cannot be read clearly in the same composition.
- Do not emit FACS/AU codes, acting theory, adjective stacks, contradictory facial movements or dialogue the user did not request.

End on a readable emotional residue that can connect to an externally planned neighboring clip.
